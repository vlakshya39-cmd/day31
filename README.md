# 🚚 Day 31 – AI Supply Chain Control Tower

## 📌 Project Overview

Today I built an interactive **AI Supply Chain Control Tower** simulation that places users in the role of a Head of Operations responsible for managing disruptions across a global supply chain network.

The application simulates real-world operational incidents, requires quick decision-making, and demonstrates how different actions affect key business KPIs such as service levels, customer satisfaction, inventory health, transportation efficiency, operating costs, and revenue protection.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Real-Time Operations Dashboard
- Service Level Monitoring
- Customer Satisfaction Tracking
- Inventory Health Metrics
- Transportation Efficiency Analysis
- Operating Cost Management
- Revenue Protection Monitoring
- Performance Score Tracking
- Live Shift Timer

### 🚨 Incident Management System
The simulator dynamically generates operational disruptions including:

- Port Congestion
- Supplier Delays
- Truck Breakdowns
- Inventory Shortages
- Customs Delays
- Demand Spikes
- Machine Failures
- Weather Disruptions
- Inventory Count Errors
- Damaged Shipments

### 🎯 Decision-Making Engine
Users can respond to incidents using actions such as:

- Expedite Shipment
- Use Backup Supplier
- Reroute Transportation
- Increase Production
- Transfer Inventory
- Approve Air Freight
- Delay Decision
- Ignore Incident

Each decision produces unique KPI impacts and affects the final score.

### 📈 KPI Impact Tracking
- Real-time KPI updates
- Dynamic score calculations
- Cost and revenue adjustments
- Service-level impact analysis
- Customer satisfaction effects

### 🏆 Performance Evaluation
At the end of the simulation, users receive:

- Final Performance Score
- Operations Grade
- Correct vs Incorrect Decisions
- KPI Summary Dashboard
- Performance Feedback

## 🎓 Key Learnings

- Supply chain visibility is critical for operational success.
- Rapid incident prioritization helps reduce business impact.
- Different corrective actions create different KPI outcomes.
- Small operational disruptions can trigger significant downstream effects.
- Data-driven decision-making improves supply chain resilience.

## 🚀 Challenge Objective

Build an AI-powered operational control tower that simulates real-world supply chain disruptions, decision-making workflows, KPI monitoring, and performance evaluation within a single interactive dashboard.
