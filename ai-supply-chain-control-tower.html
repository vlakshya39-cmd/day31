How to run the tower

  • ◎KPIs at the top reflect the overall health of your network. Green bars are healthy, red bars need attention.
  • ⚡Incidents appear on the left with a priority (Critical / High / Medium) and a countdown bar.
  • ▶Actions below each incident resolve it. Match the action to the problem — a well-matched response protects KPIs and boosts score; a poor match or an "Ignore" damages them.
  • ⏱Delay Decision buys a little time but the incident's risk keeps compounding while it waits.
  • ◈Ops Log on the right records every decision and its consequence, including effects that land a few seconds later.
